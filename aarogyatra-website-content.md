# PAGE 4: FOR INTERNATIONAL PATIENTS

**Headline:** Your Journey, Step by Step — We Handle Everything

## Before You Travel
- **Free medical opinion:** Send reports via WhatsApp or our form; receive a doctor-reviewed plan and cost estimate within 48 hours.
- **Medical visa (e-Medical Visa):** We provide the hospital invitation letter and guide you through India's e-Medical Visa application. Attendant visas for up to 2 family members.
- **Travel planning:** Guidance on flights (direct options from Nairobi, London, Toronto to Ahmedabad), what to pack, currency, and insurance.

## When You Arrive
- **Airport pickup:** Your Aarogyatra coordinator meets you at Ahmedabad (or Surat/Vadodara) airport.
- **Accommodation:** Verified guesthouses and hotels near the hospital for every budget, including options with kitchens for families.
- **Food:** Vegetarian, halal, and home-style East African–friendly meals available. Just tell us your preference.
- **Language:** Coordinators fluent in English, Hindi, and Gujarati; translation support arranged where needed.
- **SIM card, currency exchange, local transport** — arranged on day one.

## During Treatment
- A dedicated coordinator attends key hospital appointments with you.
- Daily updates to your family back home (with your consent).
- Optional wellness support: yoga, nutritionist, counsellor.

## After You Return Home
- **Aarogyatra Aftercare:** 8 weeks of structured WhatsApp follow-up with our medical team — medication schedules, recovery milestones, and a direct line for questions.
- Digital copies of all medical records, discharge summaries, and invoices.
- Coordination with your local doctor for continued care.
